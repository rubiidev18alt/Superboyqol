# Super Boy QoL 1.0 — initial release

[Download the standalone Master System ROM](../releases/superboy-v1.0.sms) (256 KiB). **1** jumps; release early for a short hop. Hold **2** to run, or press it to fire with the flower.

## What changed from RC7

- **Run-up and coast.** Horizontal speed builds in even increments from rest. Let go from a full run and the player coasts about two tiles; press the opposite direction to skid about one tile before turning. This is inspired by SMB1, not a transplant of NES physics. The existing run cap still gives 72 pixels in 32 gameplay ticks on open ground.
- **Jump and fall.** Tap and held jumps retain their 19- and 79-pixel heights. On release or at the apex, downward speed starts in smaller increments before building to terminal velocity. Each downward pixel still gets its own floor probe.
- **Wall and pit collisions.** A solid wall cancels speed and subpixel carry on the blocked axis instead of storing momentum against it. Below the bottom tile row, a floor probe returns open space instead of reading the RAM after the map. A powered player who falls into a pit loses one life and respawns small instead of taking a shrink hit below the screen.

1.0 includes the balanced Generic layouts, native scrolling, shared sprite colours, pulsing question blocks, fixed mushroom and flower drops, press-only fireballs and their knockback, and star invincibility with the three-voice **Comet Run** theme. See the [RC feature notes and clips](polish.md) for the inherited features. The stock reconstruction, v0.3.1 and RC7 remain separate builds.

## Reproduce

With GNU Make, Python 3 and SjASMPlus 1.21.1:

```sh
make release                       # build/superboy-v1.0.sms
sha256sum build/superboy-v1.0.sms
```

Expected SHA-256: `78dfa33d04db087641b7a29e9f1f01c1fee4e9206ac4c0767e9b6d8a569112c0`.

`make release-test GPGX=/path/to/Genesis-Plus-GX` compares the published ROM with a fresh build and runs cold-boot controller tests. Use the [pinned Linux libretro core](playthrough.md#reproduce-on-linux). `make verify` still tests the unmodified game, and `make polish-test` still targets RC7.

The 1.0 tests measure acceleration from rest, neutral coast, reversal, full-speed running, both jump heights and landing. Separate recordings hit a solid block at speed and take a mushroom into a pit, collect a fire flower and launch an enemy, and knock out an enemy during a star before taking damage after expiry. Verification reads emulator RAM and video; it does not inject game state. No physical-console testing is claimed.

On the pinned NTSC-U core, [`v1-all-stages.pad`](../tests/v1-all-stages.pad) clears 4-4 on frame **25,654** and ends back in 1-1 on frame **25,670**, with the original five lives. The separate idle test reaches five timeouts, game over, and attract mode.
